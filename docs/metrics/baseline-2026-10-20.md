## How to run
Paste each statement of `scripts/baseline-metrics.sql` into the Supabase SQL editor (or the Lovable `query_database`
tool) one at a time; all are SELECT-only.

## Privacy
**No query selects `source`, `value` or `metadata` as output columns** (Q-finding only tests key existence in
`metadata`). `artifacts.source` on finding rows holds free-text subject names, emails, phones and addresses; see
Phase 3 hygiene in the plan.

## Caveats
- **Confirmed-negative classification cannot be settled from `outcome` counts.** `ok` vs `empty` totals do not
  show whether a confirmed negative lookup was labelled `ok`. That needs a per-tool sample joined to the
  tool's declared negative shape; deferred to the plan (Phase 1 prerequisite check).
- `error` is computed as `stop_reason='stream_error' OR error_message IS NOT NULL`; the prompt's definition
  may differ slightly, but the figure matches.
- Test-suite counts in the prompt (680 vitest / 223 Deno) are stale; CLAUDE.md's recorded baselines are
  ~2560 vitest / ~2316 edge. Re-measure at the start of Phase 1 rather than trusting either.
